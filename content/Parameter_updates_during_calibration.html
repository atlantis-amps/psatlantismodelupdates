Parameter Updates During Calibration Process

Overview

The calibration process involved substantial structural and parametric modifications to improve model stability and ecological realism. A heuristic, multi-objective evolutionary algorithm was used to calibrate the model in a semi-automated way.

Calibration Phases

No-Fishing Calibration Phase

During the no-fishing calibration phase, calibration targeted observed biomass and weight-at-age data. The following parameters were calibrated:

Parameter Groups
mum All species
BHalpha Fish groups
KDENR Marine mammals and seabirds
mQ Zooplankton

Fishing Activation Phase

When the fishing process was activated, catch data was added as a calibration target, and mFC (fishing mortality) was calibrated for all exploited groups.

Manual Adjustments

Between automated calibration runs, targeted manual adjustments were made to improve emergent ecosystem dynamics. These included:

1. Maturation Ogives

Maturation ogives were modified to prevent unrealistic population growth in species using a “recruits-per-adult” reproduction option. In particular, reproduction was disabled for the first age class to avoid numerical instabilities caused by exponential recruitment feedback.

2. Benthic Species Spatial Distribution

Benthic species spatial distribution was corrected by activating a model flag that enforces habitat mapping based on input data, ensuring consistency with spatial constraints.

3. Predation Mortality Rates

Predation mortality rates (mQ) were initially set to zero for all groups, except juvenile stages of age-structured species, for which mQ remained active to prevent uncontrolled demographic expansion during calibration.

4. Starvation Mortality Responses

Starvation mortality responses were enhanced by increasing the sensitivity to food limitation:

  • Kthreshm was increased (from X to X)
  • mStrav was also raised (from X to X)
  • flag_shrink parameter was set to 1, enabling individuals to lose structural or reserve biomass under starvation conditions
